# Legacy — historical exploratory documents (December 2025)

The material in this folder predates the peer-reviewed version of this work and
is preserved for transparency of the research record only. **It is superseded**
by the published paper:

> Slama, J. E. *A non-monotonic environmental trend in Baryonic Tully–Fisher
> residuals: empirical evidence and a two-field phenomenological
> interpretation.* Scientific Reports (2026, accepted).

Specifically, the following claims made in these documents did **not** survive
subsequent scrutiny and should not be cited as findings:

- The derivation of the QO+R coupling from Type IIB string compactification
  (`Paper3-ToE/`): first-principles computation yields couplings many orders of
  magnitude below O(1); the published paper treats the two-field structure as
  purely phenomenological.
- The "hidden conservation law" ρ·G_eff = const and its Noether derivation
  (`Paper4-QOR-Validation/`): the underlying SO(2) rotation is not a symmetry
  of the QO+R Lagrangian.
- Multi-scale universality of λ_QR and the associated 26σ significance claims:
  replaced in the published analysis by isolation-controlled significances and
  by an explicit statement that no coupling constant is measured.
- The extension of the residual-diagnostic method to medical biomarkers
  (`Paper2-Residual-Diagnostics/`): exploratory only, never validated.

The audit trail of these corrections is documented in `PUBLICATION_AUDIT.md`
and `REPLICABILITY_AUDIT.md` in this folder.
